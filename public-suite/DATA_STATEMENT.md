# Data Statement

All 32 entities and all 150 cases are programmatically generated, anonymous, and marked `synthetic: true`. Names use reserved identifiers such as `SYN-001`, invented cities, generic organizations, and generic technical labels. No person, résumé, customer, tenant, employer record, prompt excerpt, or private evaluation item was sampled or transformed.

The dataset was designed to exercise eight contract mechanisms. Consequently it is not representative of a population, language variety, occupation market, or deployment distribution. Results establish reproducibility of specified mechanisms only; they do not establish external validity.

The release validator searches for email addresses, phone-like strings, private service paths, tenant/company identifiers, and known private-data terminology. This is a defense-in-depth check rather than a legal privacy determination.
